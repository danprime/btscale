# Features

You connect a BOOKOO coffee scale, a Belka PORTAL EC meter, or both. Then you press Start and brew.
The screen shows the weight in large numbers, the time and the brew ratio, and a live chart. With
both devices connected it also estimates how strong the coffee is (TDS) and how much of the coffee
was extracted. After you press Stop you can trim a late stop, work out the final strength, download
the readings, and look back at every brew in the brew log. Settings and brews can be kept the same
on a phone and a tablet through the owner's own sync server.
