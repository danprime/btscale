# Brew Tool

## What it is

Brew Tool is a web app for brewing pour-over coffee. It connects to a Bluetooth coffee scale and,
optionally, a Bluetooth EC meter that sits under the dripper. While you brew, it shows the weight,
time, brew ratio, temperature and strength of the coffee in big, easy-to-read numbers, and draws
them on a chart. Every brew is saved to a brew log.

## Who it's for

Right now, one person: the owner, who uses it at home on an Android phone, an iPad, a MacBook and
a Windows mini PC.

Next, it's meant for friends and other people who own one or both of the devices. Several of the
owner's friends have the BOOKOO scale. No one else the owner knows has a Belka PORTAL EC meter yet,
so most new users would start with the scale alone.

## The problem it solves

It started because the owner wanted bigger numbers that were easier to read while brewing, and knew
that the Chrome browser can talk to Bluetooth devices. A scale's own display, and its phone app, are
small and hard to read from a distance. Brew Tool also brings the scale and the EC meter together on
one screen, so you can see how strong the coffee is (TDS) and how much of the coffee was extracted,
without a separate app for each device.
