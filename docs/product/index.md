# Brew Tool product docs

These pages explain Brew Tool in plain English: what it is, what it can do, and how it works,
without needing to read the code. They are written for people who use the app (or are thinking
about it) and for AI assistants helping with it.

**Start here: [the brief](brief.md)**, a one-page overview of what Brew Tool is and who it's for.

## What it can do

[Features](features/index.md) lists everything the app does today, one row per area, with a page
for each:

- [Live brewing](features/live-brewing.md): big weight, time and ratio while you pour
- [Connecting devices](features/connecting-devices.md): the scale and the EC meter
- [Strength and extraction (TDS)](features/tds-and-extraction.md): estimating how strong the coffee is
- [After a brew](features/after-a-brew.md): final values, trimming, downloading the readings
- [Brew log](features/brew-log.md): every finished brew, saved
- [Sync between devices](features/sync.md): the same settings and log on several devices
- [Screens and install](features/screens-and-install.md): phone and tablet layouts, installing as an app
